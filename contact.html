Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to popular shopper inquiries.

How fast does Shaik Riyaz deliver?

All orders above ₹999 receive Free Air Express dispatch. Metro cities (Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bangalore, Chennai) receive deliveries within 24 to 48 hours. Other Indian destinations arrive in 2 to 4 business days.

How does the custom name engraving work?

On customizable items (24K gold bar pens, full-grain leather wallets, bespoke tailored blazers), you simply enter your chosen name or initials during product selection. Our master laser artisans engrave your text with surgical precision free of charge!

What is your return & refund policy?

We provide an unconditional 7-day return and exchange policy on all standard catalog items. If you are not 100% delighted with the craftsmanship, our courier will pick up the package and your refund will be credited instantly to your original payment method.

What warranty comes with Shaik Riyaz Chronographs & Electronics?

All Shaik Riyaz Chrono automatic and quartz timepieces carry a 2-Year International Movement Warranty. Shaik Riyaz SmartTech gadgets include a 1-Year comprehensive replacement warranty covering all electronic components and batteries.
